# Course 02 — Reading list

> **Course 02 of 05 — Reading list** · [← course01](../course01/index.md) · [Project map](../../README.md) · [course03 →](../course03/index.md)
>
> The books behind the courses, across the eight domains — starting from the mineral itself: a natural solid with a definite chemical composition and an ordered atomic structure.
>
> **Builds on:** course01 Appendix G (further reading)  
> **Feeds into:** course03 (each domain cites its books under F), course01 (section 9 of every lesson)

Numbered by reading priority: 1–2 first, then one book per domain (3–10), then the books that cross piles (11–14), then the deeper and second books (15–27).
