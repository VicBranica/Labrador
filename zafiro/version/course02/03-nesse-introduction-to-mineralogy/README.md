## Topics

- Crystallography and crystal chemistry
- Mineral properties, study and identification, including optical mineralogy
- Mineral descriptions, with the silicates from framework to orthosilicate types
- Appendices: effective ionic radii; determinative tables
- Combines the material of traditional mineralogy and optical mineralogy courses
