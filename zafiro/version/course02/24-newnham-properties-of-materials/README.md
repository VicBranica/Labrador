- Magnetic phenomena; nonlinear phenomena; ferroic crystals
- Electrical resistivity; thermal conductivity; diffusion and ionic conductivity; galvanomagnetic, thermomagnetic and thermoelectric effects; piezoresistance
- Acoustic waves
- Final chapters: enantiomorphism, optical activity, chemical anisotropy
