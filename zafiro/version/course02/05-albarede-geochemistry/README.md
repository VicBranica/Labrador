## Topics

- Properties of the elements; mass conservation, elemental and isotopic
- Geochronology and radiogenic tracers
- Element transport; geochemical systems
- Waters present and past; mineral reactions
- The solid Earth; the Earth in the solar system; the geochemical behaviour of selected elements
- New in the 2nd edition: stable isotopes, biogeochemistry, environmental geochemistry; end-of-chapter exercises with solutions online
