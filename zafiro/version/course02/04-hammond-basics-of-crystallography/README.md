## Topics

- History of crystallography; crystal structures by closest packing of spheres
- Lattices and unit cells; symmetry; point groups and space groups
- The reciprocal lattice and the geometry of light, X-ray and electron diffraction patterns
- New in the 4th edition: Fourier analysis, Patterson maps, direct methods, charge flipping, group theory, and a chapter on physical properties of crystals
