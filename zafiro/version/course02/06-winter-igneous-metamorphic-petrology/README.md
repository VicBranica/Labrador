## Topics

- **Part I, Igneous petrology:** fundamentals; classification and nomenclature; textures; structures and field relationships; thermodynamics and the phase rule (one- and two-component systems); major and minor elements; trace elements and isotopes; magma generation and diversity; layered mafic intrusions; mid-ocean ridges, oceanic intraplate volcanism and continental flood basalts; island arcs and continental arcs
- **Part II, Metamorphic petrology:** introduction and classification; structures and textures; stable mineral assemblages; metamorphic facies and metamorphosed mafic rocks; metamorphic reactions and their thermodynamics
