> Composition and structure together fix every property; the technique is how you read them or change them.

Each pile file has its own *Material*, *Technique* and *The clever trick*. The families below are the ones that cut across piles.

## Same composition, different structure

| Composition | Polymorphs | What separates them | Piles |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | Diamond, graphite | Pressure (diamond deep) | Crystallography, Gemology, Materials science |
| CaCO₃ | Calcite, aragonite | Pressure; biology (shells) | Mineralogy, Environmental, Gemology (pearl) |
| SiO₂ | Quartz, tridymite, cristobalite, coesite, stishovite | Temperature and pressure | Crystallography, Petrology, Materials science |
| Al₂SiO₅ | Kyanite, andalusite, sillimanite | Pressure and temperature | Petrology |
| FeS₂ | Pyrite, marcasite | Formation conditions | Mineralogy, Environmental |

## Same structure, different composition

| Structure | Members | What the composition changes | Piles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corundum | Ruby (Cr), sapphire (Fe + Ti and others) | Colour, value | Gemology, Materials science |
| Beryl | Emerald (Cr, V), aquamarine (Fe), morganite (Mn) | Colour | Gemology |
| Olivine | Forsterite (Mg) ↔ fayalite (Fe) | Melting point, density, weathering rate | Petrology, Environmental |
| Feldspar | K, Na and Ca end-members | Cleavage colours, melting, weathering | Mineralogy, Petrology |
| Perovskite | CaTiO₃ mineral; BaTiO₃, PZT, halide perovskites | Piezoelectric and optical properties | Crystallography, Materials science |

## Mineral families cutting across domains

| Mineral family | Shows up in | Signature technique | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quartz | All eight piles | Hydrothermal growth, XRD | Most familiar mineral; oscillators; silicon source |
| Feldspars | Mineralogy, Petrology, Geochemistry, Environmental | Thin section, XRF | Most abundant group in the crust |
| Calcite | Mineralogy, Crystallography, Environmental, Materials | Acid test, polarized light | Limestone, cement, neutralizer |
| Corundum | Gemology, Materials science | Heat treatment, flame fusion | Ruby, sapphire, lasers, abrasives |
| Zircon | Geochemistry, Gemology, Materials | U–Pb dating | Oldest dated minerals; zirconia |
| Sulfides | Economic geology, Environmental, Geochemistry | Flotation, acid–base accounting | Metal ores and acid drainage |
| Clays | Environmental, Materials science, Petrology | XRD of oriented mounts | Ceramics, liners, sorption |

## Technique families cutting across domains

| Technique | Appears in | Features it enables |
|---|---|---|
| Hand-specimen tests | Mineralogy, Petrology, Gemology | Free, fast identification |
| Polarized-light microscopy | Mineralogy, Petrology, Gemology | Minerals and textures in place |
| X-ray diffraction | Crystallography, Mineralogy, Environmental, Materials | Phase identity |
| Electron microprobe and SEM | Mineralogy, Geochemistry, Environmental | Micrometre-scale composition, fibres |
| Raman and FTIR | Gemology, Mineralogy, Materials | Non-destructive identification |
| Mass spectrometry (ICP-MS, TIMS) | Geochemistry, Economic geology, Gemology (origin) | Trace elements, isotopes, ages |
| Heat treatment | Gemology, Materials science | Colour change, microstructure |
| Crystal growth | Crystallography, Materials science, Gemology (synthetics) | Large, pure crystals on demand |
