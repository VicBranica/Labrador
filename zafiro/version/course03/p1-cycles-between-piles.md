> Every specimen is a stop on a cycle; the processes between stops are the arrows between piles.

## The one idea you need first

A mineral, rock or gem is where it is because one or more processes put it there — and other processes will take it somewhere else.

- Magma → granite: crystallization (geochemistry → petrology).
- Granite → sand and clay: weathering (petrology → environmental mineralogy).
- Sand → sandstone → quartzite: burial and metamorphism (petrology).
- Hot fluid → quartz–gold vein: hydrothermal deposition (geochemistry → economic geology).
- Pegmatite → tourmaline and beryl crystals: late crystallization of rare elements (geochemistry → gemology).
- Ore → metal → tailings: mining and processing (economic geology → materials science → environmental mineralogy).

That's it. Every specimen is one or more of these arrows. Learn to read a specimen as a chain of processes and you can read any specimen, including ones you have never seen.

## The arrows

| Process | From → to | Piles it links | Example specimen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crystallization | Melt or fluid → mineral | [Geochemistry](03-geochemistry/README.md) → [Petrology](04-petrology/README.md) | Granite, basalt |
| Weathering | Primary mineral → clay, oxide, solution | [Petrology](04-petrology/README.md) → [Environmental](08-environmental-mineralogy/README.md) | Kaolinite, goethite |
| Diagenesis | Sediment → sedimentary rock | [Petrology](04-petrology/README.md) | Sandstone, limestone |
| Metamorphism | Rock → recrystallized rock | [Petrology](04-petrology/README.md) → [Mineralogy](01-mineralogy/README.md) | Schist with garnet and kyanite |
| Hydrothermal deposition | Fluid → vein or replacement | [Geochemistry](03-geochemistry/README.md) → [Economic geology](05-economic-geology/README.md) | Quartz–gold vein, porphyry copper |
| Gem formation | Rare-element melt, mantle, metamorphism → gem crystal | [Geochemistry](03-geochemistry/README.md) → [Gemology](06-gemology/README.md) | Tourmaline, diamond, ruby |
| Extraction | Ore → concentrate → metal | [Economic geology](05-economic-geology/README.md) → [Materials science](07-materials-science/README.md) | Copper wire |
| Synthesis | Feedstock → synthetic crystal | [Materials science](07-materials-science/README.md) → [Gemology](06-gemology/README.md) | Synthetic quartz, synthetic ruby |
| Release and trapping | Waste → water → sorbed or precipitated | [Environmental](08-environmental-mineralogy/README.md) → [Geochemistry](03-geochemistry/README.md) | Orange iron oxides below a mine |

Structure ([Crystallography](02-crystallography/README.md)) rides along every arrow: it decides which phase forms at each stop.
