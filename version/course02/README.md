# Course 02 — Reading list

| # | Folder | Resource | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-slocum-precision-machine-design](01-slocum-precision-machine-design/) | Slocum, *Precision Machine Design* | If you only read one, read this. |
| 2 | [02-hale-designing-precision-machines](02-hale-designing-precision-machines/) | Hale, *Principles and Techniques for Designing Precision Machines* | MIT thesis, free online. |
| 3 | [03-ashby-materials-selection](03-ashby-materials-selection/) | Ashby, *Materials Selection in Mechanical Design* | Teaches you to think about materials as a map. |
| 4 | [04-horowitz-hill-art-of-electronics](04-horowitz-hill-art-of-electronics/) | Horowitz & Hill, *The Art of Electronics* | The electrical pile, done right. |
| 5 | [05-merritt-hydraulic-control-systems](05-merritt-hydraulic-control-systems/) | Merritt, *Hydraulic Control Systems* | The fluidic classic. |
| 6 | [06-fraden-handbook-of-modern-sensors](06-fraden-handbook-of-modern-sensors/) | Fraden, *Handbook of Modern Sensors* | The device catalog, when you need one. |
| 7 | [07-bard-faulkner-electrochemical-methods](07-bard-faulkner-electrochemical-methods/) | Bard & Faulkner, *Electrochemical Methods* | The chemical pile. |
| 8 | [08-hecht-saleh-teich-optics-photonics](08-hecht-saleh-teich-optics-photonics/) | Hecht, *Optics*; Saleh & Teich, *Fundamentals of Photonics* | The radiant pile. |
| 9 | [09-gelbart-videos](09-gelbart-videos/) | Dan Gelbart's videos | *Building Prototypes* (18 parts), *MECH 520* (25 lectures). Both free. |
