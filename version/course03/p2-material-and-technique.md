> The material + technique on each arrow is what makes the device possible.

Each pile file has its own *Key materials*, *Key techniques* and *The clever trick*. The families below are the ones that cut across piles.

## Material families cutting across domains

| Material family | Shows up in | Signature technique | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silicon | Electrical, Magnetic (Hall), Mechanical (MEMS), Radiant | Photolithography, DRIE, epitaxy | Universal substrate |
| PZT | Mechanical, Electrical, Radiant (ultrasound) | Sintering + poling | High electromechanical coupling |
| NdFeB | Magnetic | Aligned-field sintering | Highest BHmax; modern motors |
| Platinum | Electrical, Thermal, Chemical | Thin-film, laser trim | Chemically stable at any T |
| Quartz | Mechanical, Electrical, Radiant | Crystal growth, lithographic cut | Extreme frequency stability |
| GaN | Electrical (power), Radiant (blue/UV) | MOCVD epitaxy | Reshaped lighting & power |
| Nitinol | Thermal, Mechanical | Shape-setting anneal | Thermal → mechanical in one alloy |
| Fused silica / Zerodur | Mechanical, Radiant | MRF, diamond turning | Near-zero thermal expansion |
| Invar | Mechanical, Thermal | Rolling, bonding | Dimensional stability through T |

## Technique families cutting across domains

| Technique | Appears in | Features it enables |
|---|---|---|
| Photolithography | Electrical, Magnetic (MR), Mechanical (MEMS), Radiant | Nanometer-scale patterning |
| Thin-film deposition | Electrical, Magnetic, Thermal, Radiant | Precise composition, atomic thickness |
| Sintering | Magnetic, Mechanical, Chemical, Thermal | Dense parts from powder |
| Grinding & lapping | Mechanical, Fluidic, Radiant | Sub-micron flatness |
| Diamond turning | Mechanical, Radiant | Nanometer surface finish |
| Lamination | Electrical, Magnetic, Thermal, Mechanical | Combines properties, breaks loss paths |
| Waterjet / wire EDM | Mechanical, Fluidic | Precise cuts in hard material |
| Annealing / heat treatment | Mechanical, Magnetic, Thermal | Sets final microstructure |
| Encapsulation / coating | Electrical, Chemical, Radiant | Isolates device from environment |
