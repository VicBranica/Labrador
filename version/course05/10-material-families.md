# Material Families Cutting Across Domains

[← Course 05 index](index.md) · [← Classification Tables](09-classification-tables.md) · [Technique Families →](11-technique-families.md)

| Material family | Shows up in | Signature technique | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silicon | Electrical, Magnetic (Hall), Mechanical (MEMS), Radiant | Photolithography, DRIE, epitaxy | Universal substrate |
| PZT | Mechanical, Electrical, Radiant (ultrasound) | Sintering + poling | High electromechanical coupling |
| NdFeB | Magnetic | Aligned-field sintering | Highest BHmax; modern motors |
| Platinum | Electrical, Thermal, Chemical | Thin-film, laser trim | Chemically stable at any T |
| Quartz | Mechanical, Electrical, Radiant | Crystal growth, lithographic cut | Extreme frequency stability |
| GaN | Electrical (power), Radiant (blue/UV) | MOCVD epitaxy | Reshaped lighting & power |
| Nitinol | Thermal, Mechanical | Shape-setting anneal | Thermal → mechanical in one alloy |
| Fused silica / Zerodur | Mechanical, Radiant | MRF, diamond turning | Near-zero thermal expansion |
| Invar | Mechanical, Thermal | Rolling, bonding | Dimensional stability through T |
