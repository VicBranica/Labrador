## What they make

Universal testing machines for tensile, compression, fatigue and impact testing of materials.

## Why it's in the courses

Buehler and Wang used an Instron to measure Nitinol's reversible phase transformation in 1962. "Instron" became shorthand for a materials tester, as listed in the 1930s–50s equipment lineage.
