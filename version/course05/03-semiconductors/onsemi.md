## What they make

Discrete transistors and diodes, power devices, image sensors.

## Why it's in the courses

Main current source for many of the jellybean discretes in Bench 1: 2N3904/2N3906 and BC547 transistors, 2N7000 MOSFET, 1N4148/1N4007/1N5819 diodes. All are industry-standard numbers made by several manufacturers.
