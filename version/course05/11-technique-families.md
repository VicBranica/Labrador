# Technique Families Cutting Across Domains

[← Course 05 index](index.md) · [← Material Families](10-material-families.md)

| Technique | Appears in | Features it enables |
|---|---|---|
| Photolithography | Electrical, Magnetic (MR), Mechanical (MEMS), Radiant | Nanometer-scale patterning |
| Thin-film deposition | Electrical, Magnetic, Thermal, Radiant | Precise composition, atomic thickness |
| Sintering | Magnetic, Mechanical, Chemical, Thermal | Dense parts from powder |
| Grinding & lapping | Mechanical, Fluidic, Radiant | Sub-micron flatness |
| Diamond turning | Mechanical, Radiant | Nanometer surface finish |
| Lamination | Electrical, Magnetic, Thermal, Mechanical | Combines properties, breaks loss paths |
| Waterjet / wire EDM | Mechanical, Fluidic | Precise cuts in hard material |
| Annealing / heat treatment | Mechanical, Magnetic, Thermal | Sets final microstructure |
| Encapsulation / coating | Electrical, Chemical, Radiant | Isolates device from environment |
