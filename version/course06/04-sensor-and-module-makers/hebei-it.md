## What they make

Thermoelectric (Peltier) cooling modules.

## Why it's in the courses

Originator of the TEC1-12706 (Bench 5): TEC1 = single-stage thermoelectric cooler, 127 = number of couples, 06 = rated current in amperes. The naming is now used by many manufacturers.
