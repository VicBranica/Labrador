## What they make

Data converters, amplifiers, sensor interfaces, power management.

## Why it's in the courses

MAX6675 / MAX31855 thermocouple readers and MAX31865 RTD reader (Bench 5). These were Maxim Integrated parts; Analog Devices acquired Maxim in 2021.
