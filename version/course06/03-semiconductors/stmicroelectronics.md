## What they make

Microcontrollers (STM32), MEMS sensors, power and motor drivers, time-of-flight sensors.

## Why it's in the courses

L298N dual H-bridge motor driver (Bench 2) and the VL53L0X / VL53L1X time-of-flight distance sensors (Bench 7).
