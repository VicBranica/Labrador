## What they make

Magnetic sensor ICs (Hall-effect, TMR) and power/motor-driver ICs.

## Why it's in the courses

A3144 digital Hall switch and A1324 linear Hall sensor (Bench 2). Allegro has discontinued the A3144; most modules sold under that number today carry compatible clones. The A1324 is a current Allegro part.
