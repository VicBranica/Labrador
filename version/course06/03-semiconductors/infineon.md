## What they make

Power semiconductors, MOSFETs, IGBTs, microcontrollers, sensors.

## Why it's in the courses

IRLZ44N logic-level MOSFET (Bench 1). The IRL/IRF parts came from International Rectifier, which Infineon acquired in 2015.
