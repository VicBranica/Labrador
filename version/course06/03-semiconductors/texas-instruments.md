## What they make

Analog ICs, op-amps, motor drivers, microcontrollers, DSPs.

## Why it's in the courses

LM358 and TL072 op-amps (Bench 1) and the DRV8833 motor driver (Bench 2). The LM358 is multi-sourced by several makers.
