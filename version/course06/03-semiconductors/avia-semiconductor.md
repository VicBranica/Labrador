## What they make

Mixed-signal ICs for weighing and measurement.

## Why it's in the courses

HX711 24-bit ADC for bridge sensors — the load-cell amplifier in Bench 3. Channel A has a gain of 128 or 64; channel B a fixed gain of 32.
