## What they make

Magnetic shielding alloys and fabricated shields; holds the MUMETAL trademark.

## Why it's in the courses

Mu-metal is the shielding alloy in the magnetic pile. It was patented in 1923 by the British Telegraph Construction and Maintenance Co. (Telcon); Telcon abandoned the trademark in 1985 and Magnetic Shield Corporation now holds it.
