## What they make

Thermocouple alloys; holds the CHROMEL and ALUMEL trademarks and the Hoskins Manufacturing know-how.

## Why it's in the courses

Chromel (positive leg) and Alumel (negative leg) make the Type K thermocouple in the thermal pile and Bench 5. They were originally Hoskins Manufacturing trademarks; the Chromel/Alumel couple dates from 1906.
