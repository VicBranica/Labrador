## What they make

Fluoropolymers and fluoroelastomers — including Viton and Nafion.

## Why it's in the courses

**Viton** (FKM fluoroelastomer, introduced by DuPont in 1957) is the heat-resistant seal material in the fluidic pile. **Nafion** (a proton-conducting fluoropolymer membrane, developed at DuPont in the 1960s) is the membrane in PEM fuel cells in the chemical pile.
