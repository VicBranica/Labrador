## What they make

Nickel alloys, including controlled-expansion alloys. Owner of the INVAR trademark.

## Why it's in the courses

Invar (Fe–Ni, ~36% Ni) was discovered by Charles-Édouard Guillaume in 1896 and is the dimensional-stability alloy throughout the courses. "Invar" is a registered trademark of Aperam Alloys Imphy; generic names are "FeNi36" or "64FeNi".
