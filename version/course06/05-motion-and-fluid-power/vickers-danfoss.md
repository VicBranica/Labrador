## What they make

Industrial and aerospace hydraulic pumps, valves and motors.

## Why it's in the courses

Harry Vickers pioneered industrial hydraulics, including the pressure-balanced vane pump (1925). Vickers passed through Sperry Rand and Eaton (1999) before Danfoss bought Eaton's hydraulics business in 2021. Course05 names Vickers and Moog for modern servo valves.
