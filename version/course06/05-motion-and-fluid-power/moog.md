## What they make

Electrohydraulic servo valves, actuators and motion-control systems for aerospace, defense, industry and medicine.

## Why it's in the courses

Bill Moog's electrohydraulic servo valve (patent filed 1950) is the two-stage servo valve behind the fluidic pile's clever trick. Course01 credits Tinsley/Moog for the 1951 two-stage servo valve.
